## You'll learn

- For $t$ coprime to $v$, $t \cdot D$ is again a difference set.
- Equivalence under translation, negation, and multipliers.
- Hall's multiplier theorem: multiplying by $p$ gives a translate of $D$.
- Counting the classes: $\varphi(q^2 + q + 1) / 3e$ up to translation, conjectured by Singer (1938) and proved by Berman (1953), OEIS [A335866](https://oeis.org/A335866); half that, $\varphi(q^2 + q + 1) / 6e$, when mirror images also count as the same.

See every class for a given $q$ in the [Singer classes gallery](../../playgrounds/singer-classes/). The class counts $1, 2, 1, 5, 6, 4, 6, 18, 20, \dots$ for $q = 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 8, 9, 11, 13, \dots$ appear in OEIS as [A058241](https://oeis.org/A058241).
