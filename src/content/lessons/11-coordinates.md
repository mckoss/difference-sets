## You'll learn

- Points are the 1-dimensional subspaces of $\mathrm{GF}(q)^3$, written $[x : y : z]$; lines are the 2-dimensional subspaces.
- Counting gives $q^2 + q + 1$ points and $q + 1$ points per line.
- $\mathrm{PG}(2, 2)$ is the Fano plane; $\mathrm{PG}(2, 3)$ has 13 points.
