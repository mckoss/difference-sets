## You'll learn

- Adding $1$ to itself, $1, 1 + 1, 1 + 1 + 1, \dots$, first returns to $0$ after a prime number $p$ of steps: the *characteristic*.
- The multiples of $1$ form a copy of $\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}$, the *prime subfield*; when the field has $p$ elements, $k \mapsto k \cdot 1$ is an isomorphism.
- Every finite field is a vector space over its prime subfield, so its size is $p^k$.
