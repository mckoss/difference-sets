## You'll learn

- The field axioms in plain terms: add, subtract, multiply, and divide by anything nonzero.
- In $\mathbb{Z}/6\mathbb{Z}$, $2 \cdot 3 = 0$ and $2$ has no inverse.
- For a prime $p$, every nonzero row of the multiplication table is a permutation, and the extended Euclidean algorithm finds each inverse. This field is $\mathrm{GF}(p)$.
