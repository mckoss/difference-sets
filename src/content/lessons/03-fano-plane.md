## You'll learn

- Points and lines: every two points lie on one line, every two lines meet in one point.
- The lines of the Fano plane are $\{0,1,3\} + j \bmod 7$ for $j = 0, \dots, 6$.
- Click two points to see their line; click two lines to see where they meet.
