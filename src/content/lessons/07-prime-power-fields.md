## You'll learn

- Elements are remainders $a_0 + a_1 x + \dots + a_{k-1} x^{k-1}$ modulo a polynomial $f$ of degree $k$.
- Modulo a reducible $f$, zero divisors appear just as in $\mathbb{Z}/6\mathbb{Z}$; modulo an irreducible $f$, every nonzero element is invertible.
- Building $\mathrm{GF}(4)$, $\mathrm{GF}(8)$, and $\mathrm{GF}(9)$ by hand, and why $\mathrm{GF}(4) \ne \mathbb{Z}/4\mathbb{Z}$.
