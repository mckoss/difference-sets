## You'll learn

- The axioms of a projective plane, and duality between points and lines.
- A plane of order $n$ has $n^2 + n + 1$ points and $n + 1$ points on each line.
- A *cyclic* plane has one symmetry that carries every point around a single cycle, and cyclic planes are exactly planar difference sets.
