> This lesson is planned. Here is what it will cover.

## You'll learn

- Vectors $(x, y, z)$ with entries in $\mathrm{GF}(q)$: $q^3$ of them, a finite 3D grid.
- Spans, subspaces, and why every line through the origin holds $q - 1$ nonzero vectors.
- Counting: $\frac{q^3 - 1}{q - 1} = q^2 + q + 1$ lines through the origin, and the same number of planes.
