## You'll learn

- $\mathrm{GF}(q^3)$ is a 3-dimensional vector space over $\mathrm{GF}(q)$.
- Its nonzero elements up to $\mathrm{GF}(q)^*$ scalars are the points of $\mathrm{PG}(2, q)$.
- Because $\mathrm{GF}(q)^* = \langle \alpha^v \rangle$ with $v = q^2 + q + 1$, each point is $\alpha^i$ for exactly one $i \bmod v$.
