Singer Difference Sets

For every prime power q, Singer's construction gives q + 1 residues modulo q² + q + 1 such that every nonzero residue appears exactly once as a difference of two of them. Each diagram places the residues on a circle, highlights the members of the set, and labels every chord with its two differences.
